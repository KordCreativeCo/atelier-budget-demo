# Atelier Budget · demo

A calm, ADHD-friendly household budget planner. This is a demo with made-up numbers, so you can explore every page.

**Try it:** https://kordcreativeco.github.io/atelier-budget-demo/

- Three simple check-ins a day, and one clear number for what you can spend today
- Buckets for everyday spending, fun money, bigger treats and your safety net
- Savings goals, debts, investments and net worth in one place
- Focus mode, plain words and a "?" on every section

Everything you change stays in your own browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

The bucket method is based on Scott Pape's book *The Barefoot Investor*. This app isn't connected to the book or its author.

The figures in this app are simple maths on the numbers you enter. They are general information, not financial advice.
